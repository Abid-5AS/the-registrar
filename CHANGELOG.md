# 1.1.0 — Release candidate · 9 September 2026

## Red-brick campus update

- Replaced gold pickups with animated green dollar bills and a campus-cash counter.
- Added consistent procedural red-brick masonry to campus buildings and office walls.

- Fair runner revisions, telegraphed boss stamps, three admit cards, collision grace, six power-ups, pooled paper effects, and distinct campus zones.
- Pause-safe notices, gentle runner resumption, fullscreen where supported, and native pointer capture for swipes.
- Strict TypeScript, formatted source, 15 unit tests, and 21 production-browser checks.
